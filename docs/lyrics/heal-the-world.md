# Heal the World

## Verse 1

There's a place in your heart, and I know that it is love<br>
And this place could be much brighter than tomorrow<br>
And if you really try, you'll find there's no need to cry<br>
In this place, you'll feel there's no hurt or sorrow<br>
There are ways to get there, if you care enough for the living<br>
Make a little space, make a better place

## Chorus

Heal the world, make it a better place<br>
For you and for me and the entire human race<br>
There are people dying, if you care enough for the living<br>
Make a better place for you and for me

## Verse 2

If you want to know why, there's a love that cannot lie<br>
Love is strong; it only cares for joyful giving<br>
If we try we shall see, in this bliss we cannot feel fear or dread<br>
We stop existing and start living<br>
Then it feels that always, love's enough for us growing<br>
Make a better world, make a better world

## Bridge

And the dream we were conceived in will reveal a joyful face<br>
And the world we once believed in will shine again in grace<br>
Then why do we keep strangling life, wound this earth, crucify its soul<br>
Though it's plain to see, this world is heavenly, be God's glow

## Verse 3

We could fly so high, let our spirits never die<br>
In my heart I feel you are all my brothers<br>
Create a world with no fear, together we'll cry happy tears<br>
See the nations turn their swords into plowshares<br>
We could really get there, if you cared enough for the living<br>
Make a little space, to make a better place

## 주요 표현

* `get there` : 어떤 장소에 도착하다 → 어떤 목표나 과제를 달성하다
    * "When do you think you can get there?" (언제 거기 도착할 것 같아요?)
    * "I'm sure you'll get there in the end." (결국에는 당신이 해낼 거라고 확신합니다.)
    * "It's not easy, but we are getting there." (쉽진 않지만, 다 해 갑니다.)
* `make space` (= `make room`) : 공간을 만들다 (가사에서는 마음에 여유를 가지다)
    * "Can you make some space for my clothes in the closet?" (옷장에 제 옷 넣을 공간 좀 만들어 주겠어요?)
* `heal` / `heal over` : 치유하다, 낫다 / 상처가 아물다
    * "I hope it will heal over without any scars." (흉터 없이 상처가 아물길 바랍니다.)
* `for the living` vs `for a living` : 살아있는 것들을 위해서 / 생계를 위해서
    * "John delivers pizza for a living." (존은 생계를 위해서 피자를 배달합니다.)
* `bliss` : 더 없는 행복
    * "Ignorance is bliss." (모르는 게 약이다.)
* `conceive` : 잉태하다 / 어떤 생각·계획을 마음속에 품다, 생각해내다
    * "Mary conceived Jesus through the power of the holy spirit." (마리아는 성령의 힘으로 예수를 잉태했습니다.)
    * "Steve Jobs conceived many great things that most people never imagined." (스티브 잡스는 대부분의 사람들이 상상조차 못 했던 여러 굉장한 물건을 생각해냈습니다.)
* `grace` : 신의 은총 / 식사 전 감사기도 / 유예기간
    * "It was only by the grace of God that we survived." (우리가 살아난 것은 오로지 신의 은총 덕분입니다.)
    * "Let's say grace." (감사기도를 올립시다.)
    * "You have a 5 day grace period after the due date." (지불 만기일이 지난 뒤 5일까지는 봐드립니다.)
* `crucify` : 십자가에 못박다 / 호되게 비판하다, 괴롭히다
    * "The politician was crucified by the media over a bribery scandal." (그 정치인은 뇌물 추문과 관련해 언론의 호된 비판을 받았습니다.)
* `plain` : 분명한, 명백한
    * "He made it plain that he didn't want to deal with it any more." (그 사람은 더 이상 그 일에 관여하고 싶지 않다는 뜻을 분명히 했습니다.)
* `beat swords into plowshares` : 검을 두드려 쟁기의 날로 만들다 (전쟁에 쓸 자원을 평화적인 일에 쓰다)
    * "They will beat their swords into plowshares." (그들이 검을 두드려서 쟁기의 날을 만들 겁니다.)
