# Business Trip

* I'm here to attend a conference hosted by Oracle.
* I plan to stay for six days.
* I will be staying at Grand Hyatt San Francisco.

## 기내

### 좌석 이동

* May I sit here?
* May I get through?
* May I go in?
* Can I recline my seat?
* Please return your seat to the upright position.

### 식사

* When will dinner be served?
* What's for dinner?

## 입국심사

immigration / passport control

### 여권 확인

* 질문
    * May I see your passport?
    * May I have your passport?
    * Your passport, please.
    * Can I see your passport?
* 대답
    * Here you are.

### 방문 목적

* 질문
    * What's the purpose of your trip?
    * What's the purpose of your visit?
    * What is the nature of your visit?
    * What brings you here?
    * What's your relationship? (동행이 있을 때)
* 답변
    * I'm here to travel.
    * I'm here for sightseeing.
    * I'm here on business.
    * I'm here to study.
    * I'm here to meet friends.
    * I'm here on vacation.
    * I'm here to visit relatives.
    * I'm here to transfer to Toronto.
    * I have a connecting flight to Toronto.

### 방문 이력

* Have you ever been to the United States?
* Have you ever been here before?

### 체류 기간

* How long are you going to stay?
* How long are you staying here?
* How long are you planning to stay?
* How long will you be in the US?
    * I plan to stay for five days.
    * I'll be staying for two weeks.
    * I plan to stay during vacation.
    * I plan to stay about fifteen days.
    * I have a one-year open ticket. I'm going to go back within six months.

### 체류 장소

* Where are you going to stay?
* Where will you be staying?
    * I will be staying in Toronto.
    * Here is the address.

### 직업

* What is your occupation?
* What is your job?
* What do you do for a living?

### 소지 금액

* Are you carrying more than ten thousand dollars?
* How much money do you have with you?
* How much money are you carrying?
* How much currency are you carrying?
* How much cash do you have on you?
    * I have five hundred dollars.

### 귀국 항공권

* Do you have a return ticket?
* Do you have a return ticket to Korea?

### 지문/사진 등록

* We need your fingerprints and photos.
* Put your right index finger here.
* Now look at the camera.

### 통과

* Enjoy your stay.
* Have a nice trip.
* You are clear to go.
* You can go now.

## 호텔 체크인

* I arrived earlier than the check-in time.
* Can I leave my baggage?
