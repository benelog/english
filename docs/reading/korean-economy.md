# 한국 경제 관련 기사

* [SKT tops average annual salary: data](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/7792)
    * SK Telecom was ranked first in average employee compensation among major Korean corporations.
    * Hyundai Motor Company came in second with 94.3 million won.
    * Kia Motors and LG International Corp. took the fourth and fifth spots.
    * SK Telecom topped the list of highest average annual paychecks for employees among major Korean companies last year.
* [Galaxy most valuable brand in Korea](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/7342)
    * Olleh, the flagship brand of KT, was named the third-most popular brand in Korea.
    * E-mart and Olleh were listed No.2 and No.3 in Brand Stock's survey of Korea's top 100 brands.
    * Kakao Talk beat out global social network service competitors like Twitter and Facebook.
* [Economic slump takes toll on online sales](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/8026)
    * The global economic slump appears to be taking a toll on local internet shoppers.
    * Internet shopping sales declined by 2 percent in February from the same period last year, the lowest monthly growth figure since 2009.
* [Summer to be 6 months by 2091: KMA](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/7446)
    * Summer would last 174.9 days a year from 2091 to 2100 in the absence of action to reduce greenhouse gases.
    * The number of tropical nights will extend to 81.9 days, nearly 20 times longer than today.
* [Music digitalization bodes well for K-pop](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/7562)
    * Digital sales rose enough last year to offset the decline in CD sales.
    * The global online music market grew to $5.6 billion, a 9.8 percent increase from the previous year.
